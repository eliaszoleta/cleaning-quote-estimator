Embed your branded Clean Estimator cleaning cost calculator on any page or post with one shortcode.

== Description ==

Clean Estimator Widget embeds your white-label cleaning cost calculator directly into any WordPress page or post. Visitors answer a few questions about their space, service type, and condition, and get an instant, ZIP-code-accurate price estimate — branded as your business. Every completed estimate is emailed straight to you as a lead: name, phone, timeline, and the exact price they were quoted.

**This plugin requires an active Clean Estimator subscription** ([cleanestimator.com/estimator](https://www.cleanestimator.com/estimator)). It doesn't create or host a calculator on its own — it embeds the one already configured on your Clean Estimator dashboard.

= Features =

* One shortcode: `[cleanestimator_widget company_id="your-id" height="700"]`
* Auto-resizes as the visitor moves through the calculator's steps — no fixed iframe height to guess at
* No page-builder plugin or custom code required
* Works alongside any theme
* The resize script only loads on pages that actually use the shortcode

= Getting your shortcode =

Log in to your Clean Estimator dashboard at cleanestimator.com/company, open the **Embed Your Widget** tab, and copy the shortcode shown there — your company_id is already filled in.

== Installation ==

1. Install and activate the plugin through the **Plugins** screen in WordPress, or upload the plugin folder to `/wp-content/plugins/`.
2. Log in to your Clean Estimator dashboard and copy your shortcode from the **Embed Your Widget** tab.
3. Paste the shortcode into any page or post: `[cleanestimator_widget company_id="your-id" height="700"]`

== Frequently Asked Questions ==

= Do I need a Clean Estimator account? =

Yes. This plugin embeds the calculator configured on your Clean Estimator subscription. It doesn't work standalone — sign up at cleanestimator.com/estimator.

= Can I use it more than once on the same site? =

Yes — add the shortcode to as many pages or posts as you like.

= Can I change the height? =

Yes, with the `height` attribute (in pixels). The iframe still auto-resizes on top of whatever you set as its starting height.

= Where do leads go? =

Straight to your Clean Estimator dashboard and your inbox — this plugin only handles the embed. Lead capture, storage, and delivery all happen on cleanestimator.com.
